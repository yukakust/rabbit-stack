# Public full-SPKI pairing panel

Pinned MIT Nayuki QR generator renders the complete SHA256(DER SPKI) as
RABBIT1:<current epoch>:<64 uppercase hex digits>. No truncated fingerprint.
The caller must obtain those public bytes from the admitted Dell-local private
identity lifetime. This renderer has no authority to approve entropy, a key,
a peer, a password, or physical pairing. Host or GATT inputs cannot be treated
as an authenticated Dell identity.

QR version at most6, ECC at least quartile, four-module quiet zone, caller-owned
bounded framebuffer. Temporary overlay after city rendering preserves world
contents. Full text remains available for an exact human comparison. Retire
or expire the actual key and clear the panel; time/epoch checks here alone
cannot detect a replaced private identity or establish clock trust.

All native C/ASAN/UBSAN/COFF tests only Yukabox. Software tests check exact full
fingerprint text, UINT64_MAX epoch, expiry boundary, alias/alignment/frame bounds,
and every pixel outside the overlay. No native whole/physical QR proof yet.
